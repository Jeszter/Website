About Me

I'm a software developer who likes turning rough ideas into working products — from the first prototype to something people can actually use.

I enjoy projects where there is more to solve than just the implementation: understanding the problem, choosing a sensible approach, connecting the pieces and refining the result after seeing how it behaves in practice.

A lot of my work has grown through personal projects, team projects and hackathons. I like environments where I can learn quickly, take ownership and ship, while still caring about clarity, reliability and the experience on the other side of the screen.

Long term, I want to keep building useful software and eventually turn some of the products I work on into things that live beyond a portfolio or a demo.

01 Prototype early I like getting a working version in front of people before polishing every edge.
02 Think in products I care about whether a feature solves the right problem, not only whether it works.
03 Own the outcome I’m comfortable taking an unclear problem, making decisions and pushing it to a finished result.
